EXPERIMENTAL DLSS NEURAL-RENDERING RUNTIME

This package includes a community-modified RTX 40-targeted NVIDIA
neural-rendering DLL. The modification invalidates its embedded Authenticode
signature (HashMismatch). The ReShade proxy and RenoDX add-on are unsigned.
These facts are separate from malware detection; a matching hash is not a
malware scan or proof of legitimacy.

The runtime policy recognizes RTX 40 and RTX 50 GPUs. NVIDIA's official DLSS 5
launch support is RTX 50-only; RTX 40 compatibility here is an unofficial
community modification. DLAA is the default native-resolution carrier, so
spatial DLSS upscaling remains off until the user explicitly selects it.

If the experimental path misbehaves, choose Advanced > Restart in DLSS SR safe
mode, or launch `DLSSVideoPlayer.exe --safe-mode`. Safe mode disables the
experimental add-on without claiming that normal mode successfully evaluated a
neural workload.

No NVIDIA, ReShade, RenoDX, or Streamline endorsement is claimed. Redistribution
permission for the supplied modified/runtime binary set is unresolved; review
the upstream terms and THIRD_PARTY.md before sharing this package.
