NVIDIA Streamline 2.13.0.0 - neural-runtime/sl.*.dll

The complete package ships eight NVIDIA-signed Streamline 2.13.0.0 modules in
neural-runtime/, unmodified and pinned by SHA-256 in
packaging/runtime-lock.json: sl.common.dll, sl.dlss.dll, sl.dlss_g.dll,
sl.dlss_nr.dll, sl.interposer.dll, sl.nis.dll, sl.pcl.dll and sl.reflex.dll.
Streamline's source and terms are published at
https://github.com/NVIDIA-RTX/Streamline.

The notice below is reproduced unmodified from license.txt on that
repository's main branch. Upstream publishes no v2.13.0 tag, so the text of
the licence as it stood for the 2.13.0.0 build could not be taken from a tag;
the MIT grant below is the one the repository carries. Its second block names
sl_nvperf.h and sl_nvperf.dll, which are covered by the NVIDIA Nsight Perf SDK
License instead; neither is shipped in this package.
