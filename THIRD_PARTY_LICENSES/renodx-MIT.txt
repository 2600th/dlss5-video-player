RenoDX - neural-runtime/renodx-dlss5.addon64

The complete package ships the RenoDX DLSS 5 ReShade add-on as
neural-runtime/renodx-dlss5.addon64, unmodified: add-on release 6.5.3 (file
version 0.2026.0917.1432) from the RankFTW/rhi-repo release mirror, pinned by
SHA-256 in packaging/runtime-lock.json. It is unsigned. RenoDX's source is
published at https://github.com/clshortfuse/renodx.
